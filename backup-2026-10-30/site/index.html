Oct 25, 2026

1. “Cloud House” by Peter Morris in London: a pink house with Miami arches

Peter Morris has completed his own house in Gospel Oak, London: two shifted residential units with marshmallow-pink walls, elongated arches and turquoise details inspired by his Miami honeymoon and the city’s Art Deco. His own house is 133 m² — an open ground floor, two bedroom levels and a roof terrace with an outdoor bath — and the neighbouring house is 165 m² with an inverted layout. Morris says his aim was “a moment of joy for every passer-by”; social-media reactions have been either fully positive or fully negative.

Oct 24, 2026

1. UAD Alamedas Tower in Durango: a faceted facade with Gothic stained-glass logic

Mexican studio Jonathan Corral Arquitectos has completed the “UAD Alamedas Tower” for the private UAD university in Durango: 7,987 m² on a triangular plan, eight levels plus a basement (classrooms, studios, meeting rooms, auditoriums, offices, a cafeteria and a mock-trial courtroom). The middle of the north elevation — facing the Las Alamedas boulevard — is a faceted curtain wall with dark mullions and triangular panes that, the designers say, “draws on the compositional logic of the Gothic stained-glass window, translating it into a contemporary urban scale.”

Oct 23, 2026

1. Shanghai Grand Opera House opens: Snøhetta’s sweeping roof on the Huangpu

Snøhetta’s Shanghai Grand Opera House — designed with ECADI, Theatre Projects and Nagata Acoustics — opened on 17 October 2026, closing a decade of work that began with the 2016 international competition. The 146,786 m² building sits in the Expo Houtan district on the Huangpu riverfront, housing three performance halls, production spaces and public areas facing the water. Its signature is a sweeping roof that frames panoramic river views and turns the building into a natural extension of the shoreline.
